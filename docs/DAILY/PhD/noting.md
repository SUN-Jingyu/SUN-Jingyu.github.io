# A note for PhD schoolarship application

## CSC 

1. Once you receive your degree award in the UK, basically it is an e-Award. You cannot leave the UK before applying for your CSC. Otherwise, you will not qualify as an overseas student to apply for it and will instead be considered as an employed worker

2. This schoolarship will cover the full payment of overseas tuition fees for the duration of the CSC funding period and a stipdend enhancement. ___bassically the stipend just around 10,000 RMB/mon .___
